# Event sources for a Nova Gorica + Gorizia culture calendar

## Summary and main findings

I verified **49 source organisations, venues, festivals or event portals** that are relevant to a music/theatre/film calendar centred on Nova Gorica, Šempeter-Vrtojba and Gorizia. Of these, **45 are core-area sources and 4 are ring sources**. By side, the list contains **26 Slovenian and 23 Italian sources**. Applying your status rules literally gives **36 active, 6 stale, 5 no_listing, 1 dead and 1 social_only**; there are no `seasonal` rows because a festival with a 2026 event on or after 9 July 2026 qualifies as `active` under your precedence rule.

The most useful discovery is that the ecosystem is substantially larger than the obvious public institutions. On the Slovenian side, the strongest combination for automation is **Kulturni dom Nova Gorica + SNG + Mostovna + Glasbena šola + TIC**, supplemented by **ZKD Nova Gorica**, which says it brings together 28 cultural societies in the municipality. The dedicated festival sites for **Glasbe sveta** and **Pixxelpoint** are also appreciably fresher than generic aggregators. citeturn25view36turn25view37turn25view2turn25view3turn21search15

On the Italian side, the small-scale NGO layer is particularly important: **ArtistiAssociati**, **Visavì**, **Terzo Teatro**, **Kinoatelje**, **Rodolfo Lipizer**, **ZSKD** and, just outside the core in Cormons, **Circolo Controtempo** all have usable web presences. ArtistiAssociati is especially valuable because its programme reaches beyond its Gorizia headquarters into Cormons and Gradisca, while Visavì alone had a dense cross-border programme in Gorizia, Nova Gorica, Cormons and Gradisca between 12 and 18 October 2026. citeturn20view0turn19view3turn20view3turn25view30turn25view31turn25view33

Two of your suspected dead/social cases turned out differently. **Carinarnica is not dead**: its own event stream contains a 15 April 2026 concert by Martin Küchen, Andrej Kobal and Gal Furlan and posts as late as 23 May 2026, so it is `stale`, not `dead`. **Maks really is effectively dead as an automatic source**: the visible `Aktualno` stream I could verify is from early 2025. citeturn25view50turn17view6

For the two Gorizia venues you singled out, the result is also fairly clear. **Circolo ARCI Gong has no independent website I could establish**; a specialist venue directory explicitly gives its Facebook page as its “website”, while Controtempo merely has a location page for the club, so I mark it `social_only`. **Mama Angela does have a standalone domain**, but I found no first-party event listing there; the domain itself failed to render through the browser during verification, while local directories and its Facebook profile confirm the venue/domain. I therefore mark it `no_listing`, not social-only. citeturn24search19turn24search15turn24search3turn25view27

A caution on `upcoming_count`: where a page exposed the whole programme in rendered HTML, I counted the relevant future entries. Where a site uses JavaScript, pagination, a rolling cinema week or a programme whose full contents were not exposed to the parser, the number is deliberately **conservative: the number of future in-scope entries I could individually verify**, not an invented total. Those cases are identified in `notes`. Kinemax, for example, exposes a rolling 9–15 October week rather than a season-long event count; Kulturni dom Nova Gorica, by contrast, exposes a long dated HTML programme through December. citeturn17view2turn16view0turn17view0

## Organisations published through somebody else's site

This matters for your crawler design almost as much as the first-party sources. On the Slovenian side, **ZKD Nova Gorica should be treated as an umbrella ingestion source**, not merely as another organiser. Its membership pages cover Nova Gorica, Šempeter-Vrtojba and Renče-Vogrsko, and its own event calendar republishes member performances. The core member lists I worked through include, among others, KUD Ni Da Ni, MePZ Nova Gorica, ŽVS Zimzelen, KD Sanje, Goriški komorni zbor, SKD Sloga, Folklorno društvo Gartrož, KUD Zvočni izviri, KPD Franc Zgonik Branik, PD France Bevk Prvačina, KPD Lipa Šempas, KD Grgar, MoPZ Lipa Ravnica, Goriški pihalni orkester, Kromberški Vodopivci, ŽePZ Rože, KD Slavec Solkan and Glasbeno društvo NOVA. For **Slovenia**, the TIC Nova Gorica/Vipava calendar is the best broad safety net I found. On 9 October it was already carrying events in Nova Gorica, Gorizia, libraries, cultural venues and surrounding municipalities, and its individual entries expose venue and date information in straightforward HTML. It should sit beneath first-party organisers in your deduplication priority. citeturn17view4turn22search8

**GO! 2025 is still worth keeping even after the European Capital of Culture year.** Its event system remains online in October 2026 and continues to expose cross-border projects and imported local listings. It is particularly useful for discovering the organiser behind one-off cross-border projects, but I would not make it authoritative when a live organiser page exists. citeturn25view39turn24search16

On the Italian side, **TurismoFVG + Comune di Gorizia + Scopri Gorizia** form a good three-way fallback. TurismoFVG caught things such as the 36th Castello di Gorizia festival and Visavì, and also reaches Cormons/Gradisca; Scopri Gorizia had the current Visavì dates; the Comune feed has local municipal/association events that may never get a good standalone page. citeturn18search5turn18search19turn18search31turn25view41

For commercial/ticketed material, **Eventim and Entrio** are useful but clearly secondary. Eventim's Nova Gorica city page had theatre listings extending into January 2027 and a venue index containing Kulturni dom, Marco Polo Club, Klub Odeon, EPIC center and Park; Entrio exposed the current Marco Polo opening. **Bandsintown** is useful as a music-only anomaly detector. None of these should outrank an organiser's own page. citeturn22search26turn23search11turn23search3turn25view23

## Coverage limits

The **core is much better covered than the ring**, but I would not honestly call the result a mathematically complete register of every cultural NGO. The main unresolved core problem is not the obvious venues; it is tiny societies that have no independent web programme. ZKD's and ZSKD's membership material proves that there are many such groups, and in practice their events migrate to umbrella, cultural-centre, municipal or tourism calendars. That is why I kept the umbrella feeds rather than manufacturing URLs for every choir or amateur group. citeturn21search15turn21search7

I was **not able to verify a current independent event source** for several organisations that are clearly part of the cultural ecosystem, notably **Glasbena matica's Gorizia activity** and some of the individual Slovenian-community societies represented through ZSKD. SCGV Emil Komel does have a real concert archive, but the archive I could verify was not fresh enough to make it a high-priority crawler. Kulturni dom Nova Gorica itself identifies Glasbena matica, Emil Komel, Kulturni dom Gorica, KC Bratuž, Controtempo and several other Italian organisations as cross-border partners, so these omissions should not be read as evidence that the organisations are inactive. citeturn23search12turn25view13

The **ring is deliberately incomplete**. I reached Brda, Renče-Vogrsko, Cormons and Gradisca with verified sources, but did not complete an organisation-by-organisation sweep of Miren-Kostanjevica, Savogna d'Isonzo/Sovodnje, San Floriano del Collio/Števerjan, Mossa, Farra d'Isonzo and Doberdò del Lago/Doberdob. One useful cross-check is that current regional programmes already show the ring bleeding into the core: Controtempo's Jazz&Wine runs through Cormons and neighbouring Collio locations, Visavì uses Cormons and Gradisca, and Brda's own calendar lists Oktober Jazz on 25 October. citeturn20view1turn20view3turn23search22

Finally, I would use the source hierarchy **first-party organiser/venue → umbrella body → municipal/tourism portal → ticketing/other aggregator**. The research strongly supports that order: the first-party calendars were generally fresher and more detailed, while sources such as Kulturnik were already lagging behind by October despite remaining useful for discovery and archival cross-checking. citeturn25view36turn25view29turn25view30turn22search29
